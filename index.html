03 A DESIGN PRINCIPLE

WHAT HAPPENS
AFTER THE DEMO?

A clever model
is a beginning.

The difficult part is making it dependable in someone else’s hands.

AGive it context

Connect the answer to the real information behind it.

BLet it do useful work

Give it tools and a path through the task—not infinite freedom.

CMake it reviewable

Keep a person in control of the important decisions.
